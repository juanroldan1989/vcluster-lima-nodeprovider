# Code of Conduct

This community welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions is expected to help make participation respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior.
- Give technical feedback respectfully and explain disagreements.
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand project decisions.
- Respect privacy, attribution and the boundaries of other participants.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized conduct and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer, [Juan Roldan](https://github.com/juanroldan1989), is responsible for moderation. Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's profile for sensitive reports. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting a private contact without including identifying or sensitive details about the incident.

Reports will be handled with discretion. The maintainer may request changes, remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation based on the conduct and its impact. This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct while representing the project.
